# Changelog

## 1.8.0
- English name: **WFRP4e – Underworld Crew**. Full English and Polish interface; other languages fall back to English.
- All texts (jobs, terrains, loot, rumours, events, roles, traits, perks, orders, chat cards) moved to the language files.
- The rumour and event compendium is rebuilt in the world's language when it changes.
- Custom rumour files are per language: `data/rumors.<lang>.json`, `data/events.<lang>.json`.
- New chat commands `/crew` and `/underworld`.
- Requires **WFRP4e – Companion Library** (`wfrp4e-companion-lib`), installed automatically.

## 1.7.0
- Loot with hot goods and terrain fences, target tiers, preparation tokens, terrains (city, village, forest, mountains) and the protection racket.
