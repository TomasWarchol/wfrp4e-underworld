# Changelog

## 1.10.0
- **Company Ledger look** (new client setting "Company sheet style", on by default; the classic sheet stays available): a war-ledger header with the company type's crest, the motto on a ribbon, the terrain skyline behind it and graphic meters for Morale, supplies, the stash and the pressure track; ribbon bookmarks on the left instead of top tabs; fonts and parchment from the WFRP4e system; an accent colour per company type. Original SVG icons, ornaments, crests and skylines live in `assets/ui/` (sprite and gallery built by `tools/build-ui-assets.mjs`). In the Ledger the Retinue tab is a roll of member cards (portrait with the role glyph, rank chevrons, Operational Experience, status as a wax seal, current assignment), and the Assignments tab is a day plan: drag a ready member's portrait onto a job card to send them (jobs that need choices open the usual dialog with that member selected) or onto a free retinue place. The Camp tab offers three illustrated shelter tiles, a large night-camp button, Morale as pennants, the larder and the coin chest; the Loot tab shows the trader as a card and the loot as item cards with category glyphs and a flame for hot goods; the Information tab becomes the Chronicle: the crest with the company's identity, the pressure track with all six levels, the patron as a letter with a relation seal, the code and notes, and the chronicle of past events as a timeline (moved from the Camp tab). The Tactics tab becomes a deck of order cards (glyph, Advantage cost, effect, the member who carries it out and a button to give the order), with the retinue on top and the still-locked orders folded into a list that names the role each one needs. Chat cards follow the same look: a mission card with the job glyph and a success or failure wax seal, an order card with the order glyph in the company's colour, and a night-camp card with the terrain skyline in a dark header; the classic style keeps the old cards. Polish for the Ledger: keyboard access (arrow keys move between the bookmarks, Enter and Space work on every clickable card and header, a visible gold focus ring), screen-reader labels on icon-only buttons and tab roles on the bookmarks, small chips raised to at least 4.5:1 contrast, the terrain skyline set against a dusk glow so it reads in the header and on the night-camp card, a larger charge in the crest, a wider bookmark column so longer tab names fit, and 16 redrawn icons that were hard to read at small size (among them the helm, the boathook, the cutpurse, the marionette for Influence and the pointing hand for the Lead token). Screenshots of the Ledger are in the README.
- **Coin abbreviations** now follow the system: GC, SS and BP in English, ZK, SS and BP in Polish (previously "s" and "d" or "bp" in places).
- **Company types**: the Type field on the Information tab now matters. A new profile engine (`scripts/company-profiles.mjs`) gives each type its own roles, jobs, wording and night events. The Underworld Crew works as before.
- **Armed Company** (men-at-arms): Captain, Employer and Infamy instead of Chief, Patron and Heat; 6 roles (Sergeant, Halberdier, Crossbowman, Standard Bearer, Barber-Surgeon, Quartermaster); jobs Patrol, Garrison Duty, Requisition, Escort, Contract, Drill, plus selling trophies and tending the wounded; 36 own night events.
- **Outlaw Band**: the Underworld Crew's roles and wording, with a Protector instead of a Patron and no city rackets; new jobs Turn on the Hunters, Ransom and Bridge Toll; uses the world's RollTables plus 16 own night events.
- **Smugglers' Flotilla**: Skipper, Buyer (no cut) and customs Scrutiny; roles Carter / Smuggler, Helmsman, Boatman, Lookout, Forger, Purser. Contraband Pickup brings contraband, and River Run carries the most valuable load to the buyer for 2.5 times its value (times the route: back channels, the usual route or past the customs house); a failed run sinks the load. The Stash lowers Scrutiny; 44 own night events.
- **Informant Network**: Spymaster, Principal (no cut) and Exposure; roles Shadow / Tail, Go-Between / Forger, Chameleon / Hedge Healer, Eavesdropper, Courier, Enforcer. Buy a Secret, Intercept a Courier (village, forest, mountains) and Swap a Letter bring documents; the documents go either to the secret-buyer or, through Report to the Principal, to the principal for 1.25 times their value with no risk; 44 own night events.
- **Secret Brotherhood**: High Priest, Dark Master (takes a tithe) and Suspicion; roles Acolyte, Zealot, Spy, Assassin, Orator, Alchemist; jobs Ritual, Infiltration, Abduction (choose the target) and The Stone Circle (forest, mountains). New members join through the GM's recruitment, as part of the story; 44 own night events.
- **Combat orders per type**: each role of the new types has its own 3–5 orders instead of inheriting every order of the matching crew roles, and each type renames and redescribes the orders it uses (e.g. the halberdier's Halberd Hook, the boatman's Overboard!, the go-between's I Know Your Secret, the zealot's Martyrdom). The mechanics are the same.
- **Night events in two layers**: types without the crew's world tables draw half their nights from their own table and half from the shared terrain events. Shared events no longer include role events written for crew roles or 15 events with a hidden crime motif. Own events can be limited to terrains, so a cult's cellar or a smugglers' barge appears only where it makes sense. Each type's own events become an editable world RollTable, created the first time the GM needs it, and the GM's Tables button opens it. Each company now remembers which events it has seen, instead of one memory for the whole world.
- The Armed Company's patrols, garrison duty, requisitions and escorts have terrain names (e.g. Gate Watch, Forest Patrol, Foraging).
- Jobs can now be described as data only (`DATA_MISSIONS`), including jobs that use up a piece of loot; assignment badges take their icon and colour from the job data. Changing the type of a company with members asks for confirmation and converts their roles. A planned job that no longer belongs to the company's type is dropped and the member becomes ready again.
- **Night events: language review** of all 1200 events (Polish and English). Fixed non-existent or wrong words (e.g. "zimnica", "podkomnaty", "zaskarbić"), grammar and inflection slips, first-person ("nas") narration that broke the second-person voice, mismatched titles, a few logic contradictions, and setting-inconsistent terms (crosses, ducats, metres, tickets, parachutes). Only `title`, `text` and `okText` changed; ids, effects, roles and chains are untouched. Worlds that already generated their tables keep the old wording until the tables are regenerated.

## 1.9.0
- **Second role**: a follower can buy a second role for 80 OXP (rank 1+). It unlocks that role's orders and specialty (the specialty bonus counts once). The GM can also set it by hand.
- **4 new roles** (11 in total): Scout / Tracker, Fence / Forger, Charlatan / Hedge Healer, Swashbuckler / Duellist.
- **39 combat orders** (was 17): every role now has at least 3, including the Carter and the Arsonist who had none; many orders are shared by several roles. The Tactics tab is built from the order list.
- **30 strengths and 30 flaws** (10 new of each); every job now has at least 2 matching strengths.
- **1200 night events**: 300 each for city, village, forest and mountains (Polish and English), with **role events** (a capable member rolls to turn a problem into an advantage) and **chains** (an event leaves a hook for a sequel on a later night). Existing unedited world event tables are replaced automatically; tables you expanded are left alone.
- Removed the **Contact the Outlaws** job: recruiting new followers is always the GM's decision (the GM's *Recruit via Chat* proposals stay).
- Rumour card: the source now sits under the heading instead of squeezing it.

## 1.8.0
- English name: **WFRP4e – Underworld Crew**. Full English and Polish interface; other languages fall back to English.
- All texts (jobs, terrains, loot, rumours, events, roles, traits, perks, orders, chat cards) moved to the language files.
- The rumour and event compendium is rebuilt in the world's language when it changes.
- Custom rumour files are per language: `data/rumors.<lang>.json`, `data/events.<lang>.json`.
- New chat commands `/crew` and `/underworld`.
- Requires **WFRP4e – Companion Library** (`wfrp4e-companion-lib`), installed automatically.
